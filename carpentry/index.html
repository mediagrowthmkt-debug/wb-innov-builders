Carpentry

From structural framing to fine finish work — carpentry that defines every detail of your home.

Carpentry is where craftsmanship shows, inside and out. We handle the full range: structural framing, interior and exterior finish carpentry, trim and millwork, and custom stairs — all by our own trained crew.

Clean joints, tight miters, square and plumb framing, and a level of finish you'll notice every time you walk through the room. It's the detail that separates builder-grade from custom.

Get a no-cost estimate

What's included

  • Interior finish carpentry — trim, molding & doors
  • Exterior finish carpentry — fascia, soffit & columns
  • Framing — wood & light-gauge metal
  • Finish trim, crown & millwork
  • Finish stairs — treads, risers & railings
  • Custom built-in & detail work
