Kitchen Remodeling

Custom kitchens designed to be the heart of your home, built with premium materials and flawless finish.

Your kitchen is where life happens. We handle the full remodel, from layout and cabinetry to countertops, backsplash, lighting and flooring, so the result is beautiful, functional and made to last.

Every Innov kitchen is planned in detail, executed by our own trained crew, and delivered on a clear timeline with weekly updates so you always know exactly where your project stands.

  • Plumbing & electrical coordination
  • Full project management
