Deck / Porch

Decks and porches built for New England seasons — outdoor living made to last and to entertain.

A deck or porch is an extension of your home. We design and build outdoor living spaces — from low-maintenance composite decks to covered and screened porches — with the structure and detailing to last.

Proper footings, framing, flashing and roofing are what separate outdoor space that lasts 25 years from what fails in five. We build it right, down to the fasteners.

From a new deck to a full porch rebuild with a new roof, we handle the structural and the beautiful in one accountable project.
